TIDEBELL
========

An original sword game on the rules of The Pirates of Dark Water (Sega Genesis, 1994).
Six islands, six stolen bells, three wardens. Bring the bells home.

RUN
  Unpack the folder anywhere and start tidebell.exe.
  The exe is not signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC":
  choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

CONTROLS            keyboard              gamepad (Xbox / PlayStation)
  move, climb       arrows or WASD        D-pad or left stick
  jump              Z, K or Space         A / Cross
  attack            X or J                X / Square
  use item          C or L                B / Circle
  next item         Up + item             Up + B / Circle
  Tide Cleave       V or I                Y / Triangle
  pause, items      Enter or Esc          Start
  Down + jump drops through a thin ledge. Options has two more button layouts.

SAVES
  The game keeps its options, best score and last tide-code in
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Tidebell\tidebell.cfg
  Write down the tide-code shown in the Lantern-house to go on later.
