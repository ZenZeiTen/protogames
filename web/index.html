How to play

Gus has one newspaper left and a dog who just chased a crow into the old Crowmere house. Walk him around and type what he should do: look, take key, open door, go to clock. Type help when stuck. Eight things in this house can kill you. Every death lets you undo it with U, and F5 saves.

Made with

Six rooms modeled in Blender and rendered for a 320×200 screen in the 16-color EGA palette with ordered dithering. Sprites and the bitmap font were drawn in Aseprite. The sound effects are synthesized PC-speaker blips, and the four pieces of music are original FM synthesis in the style of a 1990 AdLib card. It's an original game in the spirit of the 1990 parser adventures.
