# protogames
ProtoGames is a collection of game prototypes, gameplay experiments, and development frameworks designed to transform ideas into playable experiences.

## Projects

- [crowmere-hill](crowmere-hill/): an EGA-style parser adventure (browser + Godot).
- [gates-below](gates-below/): a first-person party dungeon crawler (Godot 4.7). Its rules are reverse-engineered from the Gates of Skeldal (1998) source; all its content is original.
- [vale-of-shards](vale-of-shards/): an original side-scrolling platformer with gamepad support (Godot 4.7). Its rules are reverse-engineered from the Xargon (1994) source; all its content is original.
- [tidebell](tidebell/): an original side-scrolling sword game with gamepad support, in Godot 4.7 and in the browser (three.js). Its rules are measured from the Sega Genesis cartridge The Pirates of Dark Water (1994); all its content is original.
- [thornlash](thornlash/): an original 16-bit gothic whip-action platformer with gamepad support, rewind and save-anywhere (Godot 4.7). It belongs to the genre of the 1986 NES classic it was requested as a remake of; all its content is original.
